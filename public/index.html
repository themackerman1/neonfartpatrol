How to Play

Outwit. Outlast. Outfart.

Fart on all 3 opponent houses and keep your place fart-free to win the round. Best of 5 wins the match.

↗ Move

Use WASD or Arrows to move on desktop. Use thumbstick or tap a destination to move on mobile.

✦ Fart

Tap spacebar or Fart button to fart. Hold it down to do a megafart, which will have a greater area of effect. Farts slow opponents to toot strategically!

◇ Cleanse

On your own front door, tap C or the Cleanse button to clear its fart status. Hold it down to cast a megacleanse on your house, shielding it for 5 seconds.

◎ Powerups

Two powerups drop during the match. They make you faster and come with some special bonus abilities:

Beans - adds a knockback effect to your farts.

Gas Mask - protects you from any and all farts.
